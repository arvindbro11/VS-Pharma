Our Story

About VS Pharma & Medical Store

VS Pharma & Medical Store started serving families in 2026 with one simple promise — genuine medicines at fair prices, with a bill for every purchase. What began as a single counter in Hall Bazaar, Amritsar now serves thousands of households through our online store and home-delivery network.

Our shop runs on a fully digital record system: every sale, every purchase from suppliers and every rupee of credit or debit is maintained in separate registers, so customers can always see exactly what they owe.
